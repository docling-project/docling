# Marie Curie

Physicist and chemist, Paris, France. Contact: [curie@example.org](mailto:curie@example.org)

## Experience

### Professor, University of Paris

*1906 to 1934*

- Took over the physics chair after the death of Pierre Curie, as the first woman to hold it.
- Founded the Radium Institute laboratory with **Claude Regaud** .
